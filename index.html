Our Story

Twenty months of nothing, then a rhythm that held

How four independent entrepreneurs from the same extended family found a practice that actually stuck

“Mom has an idea for how to make progress, especially in areas outside of a project owner's competency: the idea is for each of us to operate as a solo entrepreneur for our own projects and then as contractors for each other's projects.”

— Derek Scherer, relaying Julia's idea, September 11, 2020

Ed, Julia, Derek, and Jason agreed the same day. Ed's own reply named what he'd need most — accountability holding, sanity checks, “big picture” reviews, enthusiasm building — four of what later became Stewardship's five kinds of support, two and a half years before that word existed.

What followed was not a success story right away. For the better part of two years, the agreement produced frequent conversation and almost no recurring practice — an honest start, not a triumphant one.

The rhythm that actually stuck arrived in 2022: a standing Monday meeting, proposed in May, that members started calling the Heartbeat within weeks. A monthly in-person gathering — Demo Day — followed within a month, first held in August 2022 (a week late; Derek was sick).

Since then, it has held: Heartbeat activity almost every week since August 2022, and an unbroken monthly Demo Day thread since October 2023. Discord infrastructure, a charter, and a running annual theme (2025's was "No Excuses!"; 2026's is "Align!") grew up around the two meetings over the years that followed.

How We Work

Three practices, held since 2022

All three are instances of the Kinvergence framework — the general pattern we extracted from this same practice in 2026

Heartbeat

Weekly, virtual, Monday mornings. One-liners, status updates, lessons learned, Demo Day planning, open discussion. Active almost every week since August 2022.

Demo Day

Monthly, in person, first Monday. Each member shows real progress from the previous month. An unbroken monthly record since October 2023.

Stewardship

Continuous, no fixed occasion. Accountability holding, sanity checks, big-picture reviews, and enthusiasm building — offered as needed between meetings.

Kinvergence

We didn't adopt this framework. We're where it came from.

In August 2026, we separated the general pattern behind our own practice from the four of us specifically, named it Kinvergence, and published it so another family could run it without needing to know anything about the Scherer-Fraileys

We keep our name, our history, and our own charter. Kinvergence keeps the practices — Heartbeat, Demo Day, Stewardship — in a form a stranger can actually read and adopt. If you want to know what any of the above looks like in the abstract, or whether your own family could run something like it, that's what Kinvergence is for.

“Other people will benefit from setting up their own family ventures. We're making a way for people to emulate what we do.” — Derek Scherer, February 2026
