📖 Our Journey

From Isolated to Inspired

The story of how family entrepreneurs came together to build something extraordinary

🎯 Imagine this: You're a talented entrepreneur with ambitious dreams. You've worked with amazing teams before, but now you're on your own. The isolation creeps in. The accountability fades. The momentum slows.

We felt that too. As family members pursuing different ventures, we each had unique skills, experiences, and challenges. But we were missing something fundamental—the power of collaboration.

💡 The breakthrough moment: What if we formalized our support for each other? What if we created structured accountability, regular demonstrations, and shared expertise—all while maintaining our independent ventures?

In 2020, Scherer-Frailey Ventures was born. Not as a traditional family business, but as something better—a family entrepreneurship collaborative — our own operating rhythm, where individual ventures thrive through collective wisdom, mutual accountability, and genuine support.

📈 The results? Weekly sync sessions that keep us on track. Monthly demo days that spark innovation. Cross-domain skill sharing that multiplies our capabilities. And most importantly, a support network that turns challenges into opportunities.
