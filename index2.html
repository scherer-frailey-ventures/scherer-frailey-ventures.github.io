Our Mission

We're actively developing our mission statement as a group. Here are the leading candidate expressions — each one authentic, each one ours.

"We foster human flourishing."
A deep articulation rooted in the Aristotelian concept of eudaimonia — a life well-lived through virtue, purpose, meaningful relationships, and genuine well-being. Julia proposed this as the unifying theme for the website and our public presence.

The founding expression from our charter — practical, operational, and honest about what we actually do week in and week out. Less poetic, but entirely accurate.

📖 Our Journey

From Isolated to Inspired

The story of how family entrepreneurs came together to build something extraordinary

🎯 Here's a feeling most entrepreneurs know: You're capable. You have a track record. You've worked with great teams. But now you're on your own — and the isolation is real. The accountability slips. The momentum requires so much more effort to sustain.

We felt that too. As family members pursuing different ventures across different domains — software, education, art, technology — we each had unique strengths and unique struggles. What we were missing wasn't skill. It was structure and each other.

💡 The founding insight: What if we treated our family like a high-functioning team? Not a traditional family business — each of us stays independent — but a structured collaborative where individual ventures thrive through collective wisdom, regular accountability, and genuine mutual support.

In September 2020, Scherer-Frailey Ventures was born. What started as an experiment has become a central part of all our lives: weekly syncs that keep us honest and connected, monthly Demo Days where we show our actual work, and a culture of deep support that turns obstacles into shared problems.

"The people I want to help — who are helped by the work of others in my family — have needs met by those other people. Other people will benefit from setting up their own family ventures. We're making a way for people to emulate what we do."

— Derek Scherer, February 2026
⚙️ How We Operate

How It Works

A simple yet powerful structure that keeps everyone aligned and accountable

📅

Weekly Heartbeats

Regular touchpoints — we call them Heartbeats — where each member shares a one-liner on current work, a progress update, upcoming plans, and lessons learned. Short, structured, and accountability-generating.

📊

Monthly Demo Days

In-person gatherings of 4–6 hours where we demonstrate real work — not plans, not summaries, but actual outputs. A forcing function for progress and a celebration of effort.

🛠️

Shared Tools & Transparency

A shared GitHub organization for documents, plans, and public materials. Discord for day-to-day communication and asynchronous discussion. Transparency isn't a policy — it's the operating default.

🧭

Identity & Purpose Work

The practice that separates us from a simple accountability group. We're encouraged — and regularly challenged — to develop and refine our identity statements, purpose, values, and principles. Clarity of self generates better work and better relationships.

Accountability That Sticks

Regular check-ins and progress reviews keep everyone moving. There's something uniquely powerful about reporting to people who know you deeply and want you to succeed — no mutual professional obligation, just genuine care.

💡

A Culture of Demonstration

Demo Days create a forcing function unlike any other. Real outputs. Honest feedback. Repeated celebration of genuine effort. Across 65+ sessions, this rhythm has produced more than any individual member would have generated alone.

🌱

Identity as Foundation

One unexpected outcome: a deep, ongoing collective investment in identity and purpose work. Clarity of who you are — and why — turns out to be one of the most practical things a collaborative can offer its members.

🌟 What Drives Us

Our Core Values

The principles that emerged from living this model — not written once and filed away, but actively practiced

🏠

Family as Foundation

Family bonds aren't an obstacle to entrepreneurial ambition — they're the platform for it. Our deepest relationships are the source of our most honest feedback, strongest support, and most durable accountability. We invest in those relationships deliberately.

🔍

Integrity & Transparency

We show our actual work — successes and struggles both. Honest communication about where we are creates the trust that makes collaboration possible. Integrity isn't a guideline; it's the operating default that makes everything else work.

🤲

Mutual Support & Flourishing

We genuinely want each other to thrive — not merely succeed in a narrow professional sense, but flourish across every dimension of life. Your wins are celebrated. Your obstacles become shared problems. This isn't transactional; it flows from care.

🧭

Purpose-Driven Growth

We believe that clarity of identity and purpose produces better work and better lives. We don't just support each other's projects — we support each other's ongoing development as people. Growth without direction is just motion; growth with purpose is momentum.
