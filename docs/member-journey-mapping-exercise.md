# SFV Member Journey Mapping — A 20-Minute Exercise

*Optional, disposable worksheet. If this doesn't go anywhere, delete the file — nothing else depends on it. See `DEVELOPMENT.md` → "Open question: does SFV need its own documented user journeys?" for the context this came from.*

## What this is

A quick way for each SFV member to check one specific thing: **if someone remembers *you* — because Julia (or Derek, or Ed, or Jason) mentioned you — and goes looking for you on the SFV site, does it work?**

This isn't a request to read a framework or fill out a formal spec. It's: open the live site (or a draft — ask Ed which to use), pretend you're that person for five minutes, and write down what actually happens. Ed did this for himself and found a real, fixable gap ("Ventures" in the nav didn't read as "people, by name" — now fixed to "Members" in the drafts). The bar is that low.

## The walk

1. **Pick your "someone remembers me" moment.** A real or plausible one — someone at an event, a call, a family gathering — who heard about *you* specifically, not SFV in general.
2. **Land on the SFV site as that person.** What do you see first? Does anything on the first screen suggest "there are people here, and you can find them"?
3. **Try to find yourself.** Use the nav (or scroll, if on a phone). How many steps did it take? Did anything mislead you or make you hesitate?
4. **Look at your own card once you get there.** Does it say enough about you that this person — who doesn't yet know you — would feel like they found the right person? Is there a next step for them (a link, a way to reach you)?
5. **Write down anything that snagged.** Even something small. Especially something small — those are the easiest to fix.
